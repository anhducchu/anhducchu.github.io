Hi, I'm Chu Anh Đức — you can call me Duc.

I'm a BSc student in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at [**Hanoi University of Science and Technology**](https://hust.edu.vn/), Elite Technology Program, and an undergraduate researcher at [**AI4LIFE Lab**](https://ai4life.hust.edu.vn/), advised by Assoc. Prof. **Nguyen Phi Le**.

I work on generative models where supervision is missing, unpaired, or badly imbalanced: physics-aware diffusion for CT-free attenuation correction in cardiac SPECT, optimal transport for distilling across unpaired modalities, and the theory of why diffusion models fail on rare classes.

I'm applying for graduate study abroad. Always happy to chat — about research or anything else.
