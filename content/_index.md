Hi, I’m Chu Anh Đức — you can call me Duc.

I'm a BSc student in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at [**Hanoi University of Science and Technology**](https://hust.edu.vn), Elite Technology Program, and an undergraduate researcher at **AI4LIFE Lab**, advised by Assoc. Prof. **Nguyen Phi Le**.

I work on generative models for medical imaging and cross-modal representation learning. I'm drawn to diffusion models, optimal transport, and bilevel optimization — especially where supervision is missing or unpaired: CT-free attenuation correction in cardiac imaging, or distilling knowledge across modalities that were never observed together.

I'm currently applying for graduate study abroad. Always happy to chat — about research or anything else.
