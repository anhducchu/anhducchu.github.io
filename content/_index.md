Hi, I'm Chu Anh Đức — you can call me Duc.

I'm a BSc student in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at [**Hanoi University of Science and Technology**](https://hust.edu.vn/), Elite Technology Program, and an undergraduate researcher at [**AI4LIFE Lab**](https://ai4life.hust.edu.vn/), advised by Assoc. Prof. **Nguyen Phi Le**.

I work on generative models in settings where supervision is missing, unpaired, or badly imbalanced. Right now that means three threads. I build physics-aware diffusion models for medical imaging, aiming at CT-free attenuation correction for cardiac SPECT. I use optimal transport and bilevel optimization to distill knowledge between modalities that were never observed together, such as audio and vision. And I work on the theory of long-tailed generation, deriving lower bounds on tail-class error for conditional diffusion models and characterizing when classifier-free guidance provably fails on rare classes.

I'm currently applying for graduate study abroad. Always happy to chat — about research or anything else.
