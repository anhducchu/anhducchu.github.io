**AI4LIFE Lab, Hanoi University of Science and Technology**  
Undergraduate Researcher  
*2024 – Present*
- Supervisor: Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Phi Le
- Main research topics: medical image computing, cross-modal representation learning, diffusion models, optimal transport
- Investigated SPECT acquisition physics to develop a CT-free attenuation correction method for myocardial perfusion imaging
- Implemented diffusion and GAN-based baselines for NAC-to-AC image translation on 73,680 cardiac PET slices, improving RMSE by 13.83% over state of the art
- Co-designed the first cross-modal knowledge distillation framework for unpaired audio-visual data, aligning feature and prediction distributions with bilevel optimization and Wasserstein optimal transport
- Validated theoretical bounds across 4 benchmarks (VGGSound, 200,000+ videos, ViT up to 300M parameters): 14.3% over cross-entropy and 7.5% over feature distillation on all 8 unpaired tasks, first on 4 of 6 paired tasks
